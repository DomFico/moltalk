## No professional advice; check important results

MolTalk computes structures, names, stereochemistry, 3D models and properties with established open-source software
(RDKit, OPSIN) and public data (PubChem, Wikidata), and it reports when it cannot verify something. Results can still
be incomplete or wrong. 3D models are calculated for visualisation and are not measured structures. Do not rely on
MolTalk alone for safety-critical, medical, regulatory, manufacturing or other high-stakes decisions; check important
results against authoritative sources. MolTalk does not give medical, safety or legal advice.
